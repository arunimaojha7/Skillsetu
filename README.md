# Skillsetu
Project proposal for AICW Fellowship


Many Non-STEM graduates develop useful abilities through academic projects, tutoring,
volunteering, student clubs, internships, part-time work, family responsibilities and community
activities. However, these experiences are not always described in the language used in job
descriptions. As a result, graduates may overlook skills they already possess, struggle to explain
their experience to employers, or find it difficult to identify entry-level roles that fit their
capabilities.

SkillSetu proposes a practical, AI-assisted employability workflow that helps Non-STEM students
and recent graduates translate everyday experiences into clearly described, evidence-supported
skills. Users will enter short descriptions of their experiences through a structured form. An AI
tool will suggest relevant skills and explain which parts of the user's description support each
suggestion. Users will review, edit or reject these suggestions before they are used.

The confirmed skills will then be compared with a curated collection of approximately 50–100
genuine entry-level job descriptions collected from employer career pages and reputable job
portals. The prototype will recommend relevant role families, explain the basis of each match,
identify skill gaps and connect users to suitable free or accessible learning resources. A
downloadable Skills Passport will summarise the user's confirmed skills, supporting examples,
potential role matches and suggested next steps.
